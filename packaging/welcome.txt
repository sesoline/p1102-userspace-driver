Welcome to the HP LaserJet Pro P1102 / P1102w driver installer.

This installs a native, open-source print driver (foo2zjs, adapted for
macOS) plus a background helper that uploads the required engine
firmware to the printer every time it is connected over USB.

How to print after installing:

  1. Connect the printer to your Mac with a USB cable and turn it on.
  2. Wait about 15 seconds. A background helper uploads the printer
     firmware and the printer restarts itself (you will hear it).
  3. macOS detects the printer and creates a queue automatically,
     using this driver.
  4. Print from any app, choosing the queue whose driver is
     "HP LaserJet Pro P1102 Native (str4ngemd)".

Notes:
  - Works with both Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) and Intel Macs.
  - If macOS shows "unidentified developer", open System Settings >
    Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway".
  - To remove the driver: sudo bash
    /Library/Printers/foo2zjs-str4ngemd/uninstall-p1102-driver.sh
